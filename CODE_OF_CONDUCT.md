# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the **Contributor Covenant, version 2.1**, as our code
of conduct:

- Full text: <https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>
- Translations: <https://www.contributor-covenant.org/translations>

In short: be respectful, welcoming and constructive. Harassment,
discrimination or personal attacks of any kind are not acceptable in any
project space (issues, pull requests, discussions, commits).

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported privately to the project
maintainer at **k.m.a.aarts@tue.nl**. All complaints will be reviewed and
investigated promptly and fairly. The project team is obligated to maintain
confidentiality with regard to the reporter of an incident.

Consequences for violations may include a warning, temporary or permanent ban
from project spaces, at the discretion of the maintainers.
