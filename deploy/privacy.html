Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2024

1. Information We Collect

We only collect information you voluntarily provide when contacting us via email or through our contact forms. This may include:

  • Your name and email address
  • Your institution or organization name
  • Any additional information you choose to provide

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect solely for the following purposes:

  • To respond to your inquiries about Remote Labs
  • To provide information about our services
  • To improve our website and services based on your feedback

We do not use your information for marketing purposes or share it with third parties.

3. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to outside parties. This does not include trusted third parties who assist us in operating our website or servicing you, so long as those parties agree to keep this information confidential.

We may release information when we believe release is appropriate to comply with the law, enforce our site policies, or protect ours or others' rights, property, or safety.

4. Data Security

We implement a variety of security measures to maintain the safety of your personal information. These include:

  • Secure data transmission using HTTPS
  • Regular security audits and updates
  • Limited access to personal data
  • Encryption of sensitive information

However, please note that no method of transmission over the Internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

5. Cookies and Tracking

This website does not set any cookies.

We use Umami analytics to collect anonymous usage statistics to help us understand how our website is being used and to improve the user experience. Umami is a privacy-focused analytics service that:

  • Does not collect any personally identifiable information
  • Does not use tracking cookies
  • Does not track users across websites
  • Respects Do Not Track browser settings

Analytics data is collected automatically without requiring consent, as no cookies are set and no personally identifiable information is collected. You can opt out of analytics by enabling Do Not Track in your browser.

6. Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Right to Access: You can request copies of your personal data.
  • Right to Rectification: You can request that we correct any information you believe is inaccurate.
  • Right to Erasure: You can request that we erase your personal data, under certain conditions.
  • Right to Restrict Processing: You can request that we restrict the processing of your personal data, under certain conditions.
  • Right to Object to Processing: You can object to our processing of your personal data, under certain conditions.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at: k.m.a.aarts@tue.nl

7. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of these sites. We encourage you to read the privacy policies of any website you visit.

8. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to individuals under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16. If we become aware that we have collected personal information from a child under age 16 without verification of parental consent, we will take steps to remove that information.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this policy.

You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes. Changes to this Privacy Policy are effective when they are posted on this page.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us:
