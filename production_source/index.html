Enabling students and professionals to conduct real lab experiments remotely

The Remote Labs Platform facilitates a network of connected labs that puts real physical experiments within reach of anyone, anywhere, at any time. Not simulations, not substitutes: genuine lab work with real, physical experimental setups, made accessible as shared infrastructure across institutions and borders through our platform.
