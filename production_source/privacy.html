Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Who We Are (Data Controller)

The controller for the processing of your personal data is:

Eindhoven University of Technology (TU/e)
Den Dolech 2, 5612 AZ Eindhoven
The Netherlands

For questions about this policy or our processing of personal data, contact us at k.m.a.aarts@tue.nl. You can also reach the TU/e Data Protection Officer through the same address, marked “Data Protection Officer”.

2. Information We Collect

Information you provide: when you contact us by email, we receive the information you choose to share - typically your name, email address, institution or organization, and the content of your message.

Analytics (only with your consent): if you accept analytics in the consent banner on the home page, the site loads Umami, a cookieless analytics service. It collects page URL, referrer, browser and operating system, device type, and country (derived from your IP address, which is not stored). The Umami tracker does not use cookies, no personally identifiable information is collected, and users are not tracked across websites.

3. Purposes and Legal Basis

  • Responding to your inquiries and providing information about our services: necessary to take steps at your request prior to a contract, or our legitimate interest in answering questions (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b) and (f)).
  • Analytics to understand and improve how the website is used: your explicit consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)), given via the consent banner and withdrawable at any time (see section 8).

We do not use your information for marketing purposes, and we do not sell it or share it with third parties for their own purposes.

4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

This website does not set any cookies and does not use web beacons or similar tracking technologies. The only information stored on your device is your consent choice itself (in localStorage), which is strictly necessary to remember your preference and is exempt from consent under ePrivacy Art. 5(3).

If you enable Do Not Track (DNT) in your browser, analytics is never loaded, even if you previously accepted.

5. Third Parties, Processors and International Transfers

This website is a static site: all fonts, images and videos are served from remote-labs.eu itself. The only third-party requests are the consent-gated analytics described above and the shields.io status badge described below.

  • Umami Cloud (operated by Umami Software, Estonia) processes analytics data on our behalf as a processor under a data processing agreement (DPA), and only after your consent. Analytics data is stored in the EU data region selected for our account; the DPA covers the limited operational processing (CDN routing, security and server logging) that Umami may perform outside that region.
  • Status badge (shields.io, US): the system-status badge in the footer is an image loaded from img.shields.io. When it loads, your IP address is sent to shields.io (hosted in the US via fly.io and Cloudflare), which then fetches the current status from our status page on its own server. Shields.io states that it sets no cookies and stores no visit logs (shields.io privacy policy). Clicking the badge opens our own status page (status.remote-labs.eu), which has its own privacy statement.
  • The Remote Labs platform (remotelabs.tue.nl) that this site links to is a separate service with its own privacy statement.

We may disclose information if required to comply with the law, enforce our policies, or protect the rights, property or safety of others.

6. Retention

Correspondence you send us by email is kept only as long as needed to handle your request and any follow-up, and to comply with legal retention obligations. Raw analytics events are kept only for the retention period of our Umami Cloud plan (plan-based, for example 2 years on the Pro plan - see Umami's Cloud FAQ) and are then deleted.

7. Data Security

We apply appropriate technical and organizational measures, including HTTPS with HSTS for all traffic, a strict Content-Security-Policy, SRI integrity checks on the analytics script, and access limited to what is necessary. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we regularly review and update our measures.

8. Your Rights

Under the GDPR you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: request copies of your personal data.
  • Rectification: request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Erasure: request deletion of your personal data, under certain conditions.
  • Restriction: request that we restrict processing, under certain conditions.
  • Objection: object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Data portability: receive your data in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format.
  • Withdraw consent: at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal - use the “Privacy settings” link in the footer of the home page to change or revoke your analytics choice.
  • Lodge a complaint: with the Dutch supervisory authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at: k.m.a.aarts@tue.nl

9. Third-Party Links

Our website contains links to third-party websites (for example TU/e, partner institutions, and publications). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites; we encourage you to read their privacy policies.

10. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to individuals under the age of 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16. If we learn that we have collected such information without verifiable parental consent, we will delete it.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes are effective when posted on this page, with the “Last updated” date above. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

12. Contact Us
